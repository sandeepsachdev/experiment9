You are a helicopter pilot launched from the carrier Mother. Six enemy factories on the islands of Bungeling Bay are racing to complete a doomsday weapon. Destroy them all.

  • Bombs are the main weapon -- 2 bombs drop a factory. Machine guns chip too.
  • Hover slowly over the carrier to rearm & repair.
